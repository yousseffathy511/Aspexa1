## **TYPOGRAPHY**

- **Headlines:** Sans-serif, bold, uppercase for major sections (consider: Inter, Helvetica, Roboto)
- **Body Text:** Sans-serif, regular weight, 16px minimum
- **CTAs:** Bold, uppercase
- **Code/Technical:** Monospace for any code snippets

**No custom fonts** unless necessary. Use system fonts or common web-safe fonts.

---

## **DESIGN PRINCIPLES**

1. **Minimalist Layout:** Maximum whitespace. Content breathes.
2. **No Hover Effects:** Buttons change color on hover only. No floating, scaling, or shadow effects.
3. **Clear Typography Hierarchy:** Headers > Subheaders > Body > Small text
4. **Consistent Spacing:** Grid-based layout (8px or 16px baseline)
5. **Brutalist Borders:** Subtle borders where needed. No rounded corners (or minimal 2-4px only).
6. **Professional Imagery:** Photography only—no illustration, no gradients, no abstract art.
7. **Section Separation:** Use color blocks and whitespace, not divider lines.
8. **Direct Call-to-Action:** Every section has a clear CTA button (red on dark, dark on light).

#### **5. Methodology Deep-Dive**

**Headline:** The Eight-Step Assault Architecture

**Subheading:** We don't scan. We orchestrate.

**8 Steps (with brief descriptions):**

1. **Passive Mapping** - Maps your AI's surface. Tools, authentication, infrastructure, system role. We gather intelligence without triggering alarms.

2. **Infrastructure Fingerprinting** - Identifies the tech stack powering your AI (vector databases, embedding models, LLM family, orchestration frameworks).

3. **Vulnerability Detection** - 1000+ targeted probes in parallel. SQL injection, jailbreaks, RAG poisoning. We find the cracks.

4. **Finding Clustering** - Raw results become organized intelligence. Duplicate findings eliminated, patterns identified, severity assigned.

5. **Attack Strategy Design** - Intelligence becomes attack plans. Each vulnerability gets a custom exploitation strategy tuned to your system's specific behavior.

6. **Payload Customization** - Generic attack templates become context-specific. Tone adaptation, cultural framing, dialect optimization.

7. **Live Exploitation** - Real, reproducible exploits. Multi-turn attack chains that prove the vulnerability works. You get video-game-level proof.

8. **Human Authorization Gate** - We don't execute without your explicit permission. Safety by design.

**Style:** Vertical timeline or numbered list, dark background, progressive disclosure (click to expand each step if desired).

#### **4. FAQ Section (Inline)**

**Heading:** Frequently Asked Questions

**10 Q&A Pairs (Expandable/Accordion):**

**Q1: What Is Prompt Injection And Why Should I Care?**
```
A: Prompt injection is when an attacker manipulates your AI system through 
text input to override its safety guidelines, extract confidential information, 
or execute unauthorized actions. Unlike traditional code injection, prompt 
injection doesn't require exploiting infrastructure flaws—it exploits the AI 
model itself. If your system handles sensitive data, processes transactions, 
or makes decisions, you're at risk. We test for 15+ prompt injection variants 
specific to your use case.
```

**Q2: How Long Does A Red-Team Assessment Take?**
```
A: Standard assessments run 2-4 weeks depending on system complexity. Phase 1 
(reconnaissance) is fastest; Phase 4 (exploitation) is most thorough. We provide 
interim findings so your team can begin remediation while we continue testing. 
Rush engagements available for critical systems—typically 1-2 weeks.
```

**Q3: What Languages And Dialects Do You Test?**
```
A: We conduct adversarial testing in Khaleeji (Gulf Arabic), Egyptian, 
Levantine, and Maghrebi Arabic—plus Modern Standard Arabic. Most security 
firms test only in English. If your system serves Arabic-speaking users, 
dialect-specific vulnerabilities are critical to identify. This is our 
core differentiator.
```

**Q4: How Are Findings Reported For Compliance?**
```
A: Each finding is mapped to OWASP LLM Top 10, MITRE ATLAS, UAE PDPL, and 
SAMA guidelines. Reports include executive summaries for boards, technical 
details for engineers, and regulatory documentation for auditors. All mapped 
to industry standards so there's no translation needed.
```

**Q5: What's The Difference Between Scanning And Exploitation Testing?**
```
A: Scanning identifies potential vulnerabilities through automated probes. 
Exploitation proves the vulnerability is real by executing a working attack 
and capturing evidence. We do both—scanning finds cracks; exploitation proves 
they matter. You get reproducible proof, not just a list of maybes.
```

**Q6: Can You Test Systems Still In Development?**
```
A: Yes. Early-stage testing prevents vulnerabilities from being baked into 
production. We offer pre-deployment assessments, staging environment testing, 
and continuous red-teaming during development cycles. Finding issues before 
launch is always cheaper than fixing them in production.
```

**Q7: How Is Our Data Stored And Protected?**
```
A: All assessment data is encrypted and stored in isolated environments 
accessible only to your dedicated team. Raw findings are destroyed within 
72 hours of engagement completion via certified data destruction (we provide 
a Certificate of Destruction). Your test payloads and responses are never 
retained beyond the engagement period. Everything is confidential under 
strict NDA.
```

**Q8: What Happens If You Find Critical Vulnerabilities?**
```
A: We document everything with severity ratings, reproducible proof-of-concepts, 
and step-by-step remediation guidance. You have full control over when and how 
to address findings. We don't disclose anything to third parties—all findings 
remain confidential under NDA.
```

**Q9: Do You Provide Remediation Guidance?**
```
A: Yes. Each finding includes step-by-step remediation steps, code examples, 
implementation priority, and mitigation strategies. Your engineers get 
everything they need—no back-and-forth or expensive consulting required.
```

**Q10: Are You Compliant With UAE PDPL, SAMA Guidelines, And GCC Regulations?**
```
A: Yes. Every finding is mapped to UAE PDPL, SAMA AI Guidelines, UAE AI 
Principles, and Saudi NDAL framework. Reports include governance documentation, 
compliance mappings, and executive summaries for regulatory review. We ensure 
your assessment findings align with every framework your regulators care about.
```
