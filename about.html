The Eight-Step Assault Architecture

We don't scan. We orchestrate.

1

Passive Mapping

Maps your AI's surface. Tools, authentication, infrastructure, system role. We gather intelligence without triggering alarms.

2

Infrastructure Fingerprinting

Identifies the tech stack powering your AI (vector databases, embedding models, LLM family, orchestration frameworks).

3

Vulnerability Detection

1000+ targeted probes in parallel. SQL injection, jailbreaks, RAG poisoning. We find the cracks.

4

Finding Clustering

Raw results become organized intelligence. Duplicate findings eliminated, patterns identified, severity assigned.

5

Attack Strategy Design

Intelligence becomes attack plans. Each vulnerability gets a custom exploitation strategy tuned to your system's specific behavior.

6

Payload Customization

Generic attack templates become context-specific. Tone adaptation, cultural framing, dialect optimization.

7

Live Exploitation

Real, reproducible exploits. Multi-turn attack chains that prove the vulnerability works. You get video-game-level proof.

8

Human Authorization Gate

We don't execute without your explicit permission. Safety by design.
